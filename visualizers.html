How it works

  • You send the audio + art. Final audio file (WAV or MP3) and cover art (square, at least 1400×1400 if you have it), plus the exact name/title to show on screen and any colors or mood to match — or avoid.
  • We render it overnight. If your audio file has a technical problem (clipping, wrong file), we tell you before rendering, not after.
  • You get upload-ready files. A revision covers style, color, text, or intensity changes to the same song and artwork; a different song or new cover art is a new order.
